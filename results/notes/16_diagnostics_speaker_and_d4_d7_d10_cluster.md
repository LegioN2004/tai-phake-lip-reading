# Experiment Notes: 16_diagnostics_speaker_and_d4_d7_d10_cluster

- **Notebook**: [`notebooks/16_diagnostics_speaker_and_d4_d7_d10_cluster.ipynb`](../../notebooks/16_diagnostics_speaker_and_d4_d7_d10_cluster.ipynb)
- **Dataset Mode**: `80_20` (6 validation base speakers, 220 total utterances)
- **Goal**: Step 2 diagnostic investigation into per-speaker accuracy variances and the recurrent {d4, d7, d10} confusion triangle.

---

## 1. Questions Addressed in this Investigation

### A. Is there a "bad speaker" whose face crops are failing?
If 5 validation speakers exhibit ~70% accuracy while 1 speaker drops to 15–25%, the model is not under-fitting; rather, dlib/mediapipe cropping failed on that speaker (e.g., poor lighting, extreme head pose, partial occlusion). This notebook evaluates:
- Per-speaker accuracy across all 6 validation speakers for **BiLSTM**, **BiGRU**, **Transformer**, and **Ensemble**.
- Generates a comparison bar chart (`speaker_accuracy_barplot.png`).

### B. What is driving the {d4, d7, d10} confusion triangle?
Across all three models, classes $d_4$, $d_7$, and $d_{10}$ share high pairwise error rates:
- **Sub-Confusion Matrix**: Isolates and visualizes transitions specifically between $d_4 \leftrightarrow d_7 \leftrightarrow d_{10}$.
- **Speaker Dominance Analysis**: Identifies whether a single speaker is responsible for the majority of $d_4 \rightarrow d_{10}$ or $d_7 \rightarrow d_4$ misclassifications.
- **Persistent Tri-Model Failures**: Extracts all video utterances where **all three champion models failed simultaneously**.
- **Frame Strip Visualizer**: Renders 8-frame temporal progression strips for those failed samples directly inside the notebook to visually verify whether mouth landmarks were correctly cropped or if the mouth was closed/occluded.
